Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Everything stays on your iPhone

Places, scan results, device lists, checklists and evidence photos are stored only on your device, in encrypted app storage. We have no accounts and no servers that receive this data.

Permissions

  • Local network — to check devices on the Wi-Fi you're connected to. Traffic never leaves that network.
  • Bluetooth — to list nearby devices and measure signal strength.
  • Camera — for the lens finder. Nothing is recorded unless you tap the photo button (back camera only).
  • Photos — only when you choose to import a picture as evidence.

Camera and face data

Lumyx does not collect, store, share or analyse face data. The lens finder shows a live camera preview and looks only for small bright reflections. It has no face detection or face recognition and does not use the TrueDepth API, ARKit or Vision.

One lens finder mode (IR) uses the front camera, so your face may appear in the live preview on your own screen. These frames are processed in memory on your iPhone and discarded immediately. Photos cannot be saved in this mode. Nothing from the camera is sent off your device or to any third party, so there is no face data to retain or delete.

Analytics

This version of Lumyx sends no analytics and contains no advertising or tracking. We collect no data from the app.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
