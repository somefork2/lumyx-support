Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Everything stays on your iPhone

Places, scan results, device lists, checklists and evidence photos are stored only on your device, in encrypted app storage. We have no accounts and no servers that receive this data.

Permissions

  • Local network — to check devices on the Wi-Fi you're connected to. Traffic never leaves that network.
  • Bluetooth — to list nearby devices and measure signal strength.
  • Camera — for the lens finder. Nothing is recorded unless you tap the photo button.
  • Photos — only when you choose to import a picture as evidence.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics without the advertising identifier to count anonymous events such as "scan finished" or "paywall shown". No names, contacts, photos, network names or device lists are sent. No tracking across apps.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
