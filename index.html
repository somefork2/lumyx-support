How it works

  • Wi-Fi — finds devices that answer a video-stream request (RTSP) or expose camera ports on the network you're on. If the network blocks devices from seeing each other, the app says so instead of reporting a clean result.
  • Bluetooth — lists trackers (AirTag/Find My, Tile, SmartTag, Chipolo) and camera-named devices; the locator gets warmer as you approach.
  • Lens finder — the flashlight makes a camera lens reflect as a steady dot.
  • Hiding spots — a checklist of the places cameras are really found.

No phone app can guarantee a room is free of cameras. Lumyx tells you why something was flagged so you can decide.
